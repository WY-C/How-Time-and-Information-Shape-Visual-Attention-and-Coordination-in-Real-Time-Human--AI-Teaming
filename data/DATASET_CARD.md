# Data Availability

This repository accompanies:

How Time and Information Shape Visual Attention and Coordination
in Real-Time Human-AI Teaming.

The study involved 36 participants.

## Current release

Research code and trained models are provided.

Participant-level gameplay logs, raw and processed gaze-tracking
records, questionnaire responses, and derived participant-level
datasets are not included before article publication.

Analyses requiring those records cannot be fully reproduced
from this pre-publication snapshot.

## Planned release after publication

The final IRB research protocol (Section 19) and participant
information sheet (Section 8) describe a post-publication
release of anonymized research data through GitHub.

Planned materials include:

- Gameplay logs
- Raw gaze-tracking data
- Processed gaze-tracking data
- Questionnaire responses

## Anonymization requirements

Before releasing participant data:

1. Replace internal participant IDs with randomized public IDs.
2. Remove names, contact details, and linkage keys.
3. Remove exact experiment dates and times.
4. Remove direct and indirect identifying information.
5. Remove or anonymize identifying free-text responses.
6. Review gaze, gameplay, questionnaire, and metadata records
   for residual re-identification risks.
7. Verify consistency with the final IRB protocol and consent.

## Private research records

Original research records remain subject to restricted access
and encrypted storage requirements in the final protocol.

No participant-level data or encrypted participant archive
are distributed in the current public snapshot.
